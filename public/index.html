GET TO KNOW US

Care rooted in
our community.

The Rural Health Unit of Bulan (RHU-Bulan) serves as the municipality's frontline, government-owned health hub, delivering essential medical and preventive healthcare to the community.

◉ OUR VISION

A healthier, stronger Bulan.

To be a premier, highly responsive, and model rural health facility cultivating a healthy, vibrant, and resilient community where every resident has equal access to holistic healthcare.

✳ OUR MISSION

Accessible care for all.

To improve the overall well-being of the local population by providing accessible, affordable, and high-quality preventive and curative healthcare.
